The Olive Branches Schools have built an enviable legacy anchored on Isaiah 8:18 and academic excellence—from National Olympiad gold to historic visits by Nobel Laureate Prof. Wole Soyinka. However, the school's current public website (built on legacy WordPress) carries technical debt:

Current Limitations on Live Site

• 253KB unoptimized HTML + 31 external script tags causing slow mobile loading.
• Third-party redirection to obs.softedu.info lacking unified school branding.
• Zero direct tuition payment integration.
• Results and reports disconnected from instant mobile clearance.

Amazoe Solutions Upgraded Architecture

• Global Edge CDN delivery with 0.6-second sub-second load times.
• Unified Single-Sign-On Student & Parent Portal.
• Instant Paystack payment with QR-verifiable bursary clearance receipts.
• 24/7 "Olive AI" parent assistant answering in English, Yoruba, and French.

2. Eliminating the Bank Teller Hustle (For Parents, Teachers & Bursars)

💳 Say Goodbye to Bank Queues & Paper Tellers

In typical Nigerian private schools, term resumption means parents queuing up under the sun in bank branches to fill pink/yellow paper deposit slips, bringing tellers to the school bursar, and teachers spending days cross-referencing bank statements. Our Paystack Integration completely eliminates this: Parents pay securely in 30 seconds via Card, Bank Transfer, or USSD from home. The portal automatically reconciles the bursary ledger in real-time and issues an Official Stamped Clearance Receipt. Neither parents nor teachers ever have to visit a bank branch!
