Welcome Message from the Trustees.

Guided by Faith, Grounded in Excellence, Focused on the Future.

We, the Trustees of The Olive Branches Schools, are daily grateful to be a part of the lives of the staff and children who walk through the school gates. Our children and staff are the heritage of The Lord, and we daily confess that they will be signs and wonders according to Isaiah 8:18 of the Holy Bible.

We trust that parents of the children and other stakeholders of the School will, by the same grace of God, be daily loaded with untold benefits as they invest in qualitative education for their wards.

Over the years, we have stood for the values of transparency and an aversion to false facades in academic evaluation. The Latin creed "Per Angusta Ad Augusta", which means through difficulty to greatness, represents the heritage we hand over to our learners.

Integrated Pedagogy

The Olive Branches Curriculum

Blending International Baccalaureate (IB) Guidelines with the Nigerian NERDC Curriculum

The Olive Branches Schools operate the Nigerian National Educational Curriculum (NERDC) enhanced with proven adaptations from international curricular. The International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO) provides deep inspiration for our teaching philosophy:

IB-PYP (Primary Years Programme)

For Pre-School and Junior School learners (Ages 1 - 10). Encourages curiosity, inquiry-led investigation, critical thinking, and social-emotional growth rather than mechanical memorization.

IB-MYP (Middle Years Programme)

For Middle School and High School learners (Ages 11 - 17). Builds deep conceptual mastery across STEM, Arts, Languages, and Humanities with real-world applications and global awareness.
