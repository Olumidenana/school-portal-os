Leadership & Governance

Welcome Message from the Trustees.

Guided by Faith, Grounded in Excellence, Focused on the Future.

We, the Trustees of The Olive Branches Schools, are daily grateful to be a part of the lives of the staff and children who walk through the school gates. Our children and staff are the heritage of The Lord, and we daily confess that they will be signs and wonders according to Isaiah 8:18 of the Holy Bible.

We trust that parents of the children and other stakeholders of the School will, by the same grace of God, be daily loaded with untold benefits as they invest in qualitative education for their wards.

Over the years, we have stood for the values of transparency and an aversion to false facades in academic evaluation. The Latin creed "Per Angusta Ad Augusta", which means through difficulty to greatness, represents the heritage we hand over to our learners.

Our Vision...

"...Educational facilities dedicated to furthering human knowledge for the glory of God."

Our Mission...

"...to create and maintain facilities for the comprehensive education of childhood and children years, thus preparing them for critical consciousness about themselves as citizens of their environment, and ultimately grooming them to positively affect their environment; thus becoming responsible citizens of the global community, poised to facilitate positive change and sustainable development."

Current WordPress Site (live audit)

  • ❌ 253KB bloated HTML + 31 external scripts: Loads 5 duplicate animation libraries (GSAP, ScrollTrigger, WOW, Slick, and 2 versions of Swiper), slowing mobile connections.
  • ❌ Disconnected E-Portal: Links to obs.softedu.info, a dated third-party PHP script with zero unified school branding.
  • ❌ No Online Tuition Clearance: Parents must manually queue up or bring paper tellers to the bursary.
  • ❌ No AI Assistance: Parents must wait days for manual replies to basic admission or fee inquiries.

Our Next-Gen AI Platform

  • ✅ Sub-second Speed & Clean Code: Zero bloated WordPress plugins. Powered by modern CDN edge delivery.
  • ✅ Integrated E-Portal & Paystack: Continuous Assessment, GPA tracking, and 1-tap fee clearance directly in one place.
  • ✅ Smart "Olive AI" Assistant: 24/7 multilingual parent support answering fees, curriculum, and bus questions instantly.
  • ✅ Printable A4 QR Report Cards: Embassies, WAEC, and parents can verify terminal results in 1 scan.
