## 🏛️ What is EduCore OS?
EduCore OS is an institutional-grade, mobile-first School Operating System designed specifically for premier private secondary schools in Nigeria and across Africa (such as The Olive Branches Schools, Roseful International High School, GOF International, Grange, Greensprings, and Day Waterman).

It solves the two highest-value challenges facing private school proprietors today:
1. **Parent Administrative Delight & Brand Prestige:** A blazingly fast, tamper-proof result checking portal with cryptographically signed QR verification, digital tuition clearance, and automated WhatsApp parent broadcasting.
2. **The "Junior AI & Software Foundry":** A dedicated hands-on tech club curriculum that teaches secondary school students real AI prompt engineering, Python algorithms, computer vision, and live web app deployment—positioning the school as the undisputed leader in 21st-century education.

   * Instant 1-click branding toggle between top schools:
     * *The Olive Branches Schools (Oroki Estate, Osogbo)*
